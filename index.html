What does the map show?

The visualization approximates Human Existence Potential (HEP) using mean temperature and annual precipitation. Simplified preference distributions are combined for both factors.

The underlying climate data come from ERA-Interim and cover the period from 1980 to 2009. This map is a simplified model visualization, not a prediction of individual quality of life.
